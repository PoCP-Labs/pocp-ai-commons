# PoCP AI Commons — Vision

> **PoCP is AI-era contribution proof infrastructure** — not an AI subscription platform, but a network that records who created what value, verifies it, and distributes AI capability, reputation, and opportunity accordingly.

Another way to say this:

> **PoCP is the Contribution Internet for the AI era.**

The PC Internet connected information. The Mobile Internet connected people and services. PoCP connects humans, AI agents, skills, tools, data, tasks, reviews, reputation, and usage rights through verified contribution.

When AI drives the marginal cost of knowledge, code, and content toward zero, what becomes scarce is not capability alone, but **trusted contribution, responsibility, judgment, collaboration, and reputation**. PoCP builds a verifiable ledger and allocation mechanism for these.

## What PoCP Is

| Ordinary AI platform | PoCP |
|----------------------|------|
| We provide models, you pay to use | Humans, Agents, Skills, models, and tools collaborate; the system records each participant's contribution |
| Value = model access | Value = verified contribution → AI usage rights, reputation, opportunity, governance eligibility |

PoCP organizes **collaboration relationships in the AI era**, answering:

- Who participated?
- Who contributed what?
- Who verified?
- Who earns reputation?
- Who earns usage rights?
- Who participates in governance?
- Who shares network rewards?

## Layered Value

### 1. Universal Basic AI Capability

Base AI capability for everyone; higher capability earned through verified contribution. Not UBI (money), but **UBAIC** — Universal Basic AI Capability.

### 2. Visible Contributions

Turn scattered work — study notes, bug fixes, volunteer help, reusable templates, Agent tasks, Skill invocations — into:

- **Contribution Events**
- **Contribution Ledger**
- **Contribution Reputation**

A person proves themselves through a chain of verified contributions, not only degrees and job titles.

### 3. Contribution Credit

A new form of credit complementing financial credit: a long-term record of collaboration, task completion, public-goods maintenance, and helping others.

### 4. Agent / Skill Reputation Network

Every Skill and Agent carries a contribution history: invocations, success rate, review pass rate, tasks served, maintainers, risk records. Users choose **which Agent / Skill / Workflow to trust** — not just which App to install.

### 5. Human–Agent Collaboration Network

Tasks are completed by mixed teams:

| Participant | Contribution |
|-------------|--------------|
| Human | Judgment, creativity, responsibility, final confirmation |
| Agent | Execution, search, organization, generation |
| Skill | Specialized capability |
| Tool / Dataset | Infrastructure |
| Reviewer | Quality assurance |
| Sponsor | Resource pool |

PoCP records the full collaboration chain.

### 6. Public Goods Incentives

Open source, education, community knowledge, public datasets — contributors earn reputation → AI Credits, task priority, governance weight → sustainable maintenance without relying on passion alone.

### 7. Transparent Sponsorship

Sponsor pools → task pools → contribution records → credit distribution → usage logs → impact reports. Foundations, CSR, schools, and communities distribute **AI capability**, not opaque cash.

### 8. Governance by Contribution

Governance based on recent contribution, long-term reputation, domain expertise, human confirmation, and transparent ledger — not merely wealth, early access, or platform control.

### 9. Human + AI Co-Creation

AI participates in contribution but does not monopolize value. Humans retain goals, judgment, responsibility, ethics, and final confirmation.

### 10. Contribution Graph (Ultimate Asset)

The network's most valuable asset is not points or tokens, but the **Contribution Graph**:

```
Who contributed what
Who verified what
Which Agent participated
Which Skill was invoked
Which dataset supported
Which organization sponsored
Which community benefited
What impact resulted
```

This graph powers recommendation, allocation, governance, and trust across the AI collaboration economy.

## Commercial & Social Value

**Commercial:** AI Credits subscriptions, enterprise task market, Agent/Skill marketplace fees, sponsor pool management, certification APIs, internal enterprise contribution networks.

**Social:** Reduce AI inequality, give ordinary people a second growth path, sustain public goods, make volunteer and open-source work visible, keep human meaning in AI collaboration, transparent charitable allocation.

> Business models must serve the protocol — not the reverse.

## Risks & Guardrails

| Risk | Guardrail |
|------|-----------|
| Points farming | Quality verification + human review |
| Platform extraction | Value flows back via ledger |
| Social credit abuse | Voluntary participation, transparent purpose |
| AI review bias | AI suggests only; humans confirm |
| Early-user aristocracy | Reputation from verified contribution only; not purchasable |

**Five non-negotiable principles:**

1. Voluntary participation
2. Transparent purpose
3. AI advises only
4. Humans have final confirmation
5. Reputation cannot be simply purchased
