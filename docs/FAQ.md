# FAQ — PoCP AI Commons

_Frequently asked questions about PoCP, AI Credits, and the project's direction._

---

## 💰 Is PoCP a token / crypto project?

**No.** PoCP does not start with a token.

PoCP AI Commons is a **contribution-first** platform. The first version focuses on building a working loop:

> **Contribute → Verify → Earn AI Credits → Use AI → Contribute more**

There is no token issuance, no ICO, and no speculative asset in v0.1.

---

## 🎟️ What are AI Credits?

**AI Credits are usage rights, not financial assets.**

When you complete a verified contribution task, you earn AI Credits that allow you to use AI tools on the platform (chat, analysis, generation, etc.).

Think of them like a **gym membership earned through volunteering** — you get access by contributing, not by buying. They are:

| ✅ ARE | ❌ ARE NOT |
|--------|-----------|
| Usage rights on the platform | A tradeable cryptocurrency |
| Earned through verified contribution | A speculative investment |
| Tracked on the contribution ledger | A promise of future monetary value |

---

## 💡 Why not start with a token?

Starting with a token would shift focus from **building real value** to **speculation** — exactly what PoCP wants to avoid.

The project's core belief is:

> **Contribution before financialization.**

First build a working network where people contribute, verify, earn access, and create value. If and when tokenization makes sense, it will be a decision made by the community of contributors — not a pre-condition imposed from day one.

---

## 🤖 Can AI contribute to PoCP?

**Yes.** PoCP is designed for both humans and intelligent agents. Entities can be:

- Humans
- AI agents
- LLMs
- Skills
- Tools
- Datasets
- Workflows
- Organizations
- Communities

However, **final verification approval must always come from a human reviewer** — AI is advisory, not authoritative.

---

## 🔒 How do you prevent abuse?

Anti-abuse is a core concern from day one. The MVP includes:

- **AI advisory review** — flags suspicious submissions before they reach human reviewers
- **Human verification** — final approval is always human
- **Contribution ledger** — all activity is transparent and auditable
- **Anti-abuse rules** — specific checks against gaming, duplicate work, and low-effort submissions (see the [`anti-abuse`](https://github.com/PoCP-Labs/pocp-ai-commons/labels/anti-abuse) issues)

---

## 🆓 Is PoCP AI Commons free?

**You can earn access without paying** — but it's not "unlimited free AI."

You receive basic AI Credits upon registration. You earn more through verified contribution. The more you contribute, the more AI access you unlock.

This model is designed to be sustainable: the network grows as people contribute real value, and AI access is earned, not extracted.

---

## 👤 Who controls the platform?

During the Genesis and MVP phases, the founding team at **PoCP Labs** maintains the codebase and infrastructure.

The long-term vision (Phase 4+) is **governance by contribution** — where active contributors have a say in protocol decisions, reward distribution, and platform direction.
