Genesis Manifesto

Written for a contribution civilization in the age of AI

This page adapts the core argument of the PoCP genesis text into a static public reading experience for GitHub Pages.

Before genesis

For a long time, humanity organized the world with money, markets, and power. That system priced exchange and coordinated strangers, but it also left a blind spot: much genuine contribution was never properly seen, recorded, or returned.

Care work, knowledge curation, open-source maintenance, community repair, workflow design, and reusable skills often create public value without securing rights, reputation, or durable recognition.

AI changed the foundation of value

AI made code, content, summarization, translation, workflow assembly, and many forms of routine cognition far cheaper. That does not erase human value. It shifts scarcity toward judgment, verification, responsibility, coordination, and sustained contribution to public goods.

AI can be a verifier. It cannot be a ruler.

PoCP proves contribution

Proof of Contribution Protocol asks a different question from capital-first systems: who created real contribution, who participated, who verified the result, and what rights should follow from that event. 

Contribution
-> Verification
-> Reputation
-> Rights
-> Governance
-> Value Distribution

Contributors are not only human

PoCP defines first-class participants as entities: humans, agents, LLMs, skills, tools, datasets, workflows, organizations, and communities. A single contribution may involve several of them at once.

The protocol therefore records not only an isolated submission, but the contribution graph around it: which entities participated, what each one did, how verification happened, and how rights and reputation should be allocated.

AI Commons is the first genesis application

The first real implementation is PoCP AI Commons, a public AI capability network where ordinary people receive baseline AI access and then earn more through verified contribution. 

Contributor registers
-> Receives basic AI Credits
-> Uses AI tools
-> Completes contribution task
-> Submits evidence
-> AI advisory review
-> Human reviewer confirmation
-> Earns CP and AI Credits
-> Continues using AI
-> Continues contributing

AI Credits are the first right

PoCP does not begin with a token because speculative layers distort early incentives. It begins with AI Credits: a directly understandable usage right earned through real, verified contribution.

First prove contribution. Then build reputation. Then allocate rights. Only after that should financialization even enter the conversation.

What we reject

PoCP rejects free labor extraction disguised as openness, rejects AI final-rule systems, rejects opaque allocation by platform owners, and rejects reputation as a coercive social-credit mechanism.

Contribution must stay voluntary, legible, accountable, and tied to visible return. Human responsibility remains the final line.
