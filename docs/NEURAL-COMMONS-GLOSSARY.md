# Neural Commons Glossary

## PoCP

Proof of Contribution Protocol.

## Neural Commons Network

A distributed intelligence and compute network coordinated by verified contribution, tokenized measurement, reputation, and governance.

## Entity

A participant that can contribute, invoke, verify, settle, or govern.

## Capability

A callable ability provided by an Entity.

## Invocation

A record of one Entity calling another Entity's capability.

## CP

Contribution Points. Measures verified contribution.

## AIC

AI Credits. Usage rights for intelligence capabilities.

## CC

Compute Credits. Usage rights for compute capabilities.

## PT

PoCP Protocol Token. Future protocol settlement, staking, and governance unit.

## Compute Node

An Entity that provides compute resources.

## Neural Routing

The process of matching tasks to agents, skills, LLMs, tools, datasets, workflows, and compute nodes.

## Settlement

The process of distributing CP, credits, tokens, reputation, or penalties after verified work.

## Reputation

Contextual trust score based on verified contribution and performance.

## Governance Power

The right to influence protocol rules, ideally based on stake, reputation, contribution recency, role eligibility, and risk adjustment.

PoCP begins with contribution.
