# PoCP as the Contribution Internet

PoCP is not only an AI application.

It is an attempt to define a new network layer for the AI era: **the Contribution Internet**.

## One Sentence

**PoCP is the Contribution Internet for the AI era.**

In Chinese:

**PoCP 是 AI 时代的贡献互联网。**

## From PC Internet to Mobile Internet to Contribution Internet

| Era | What Became Connected | Core Unit | Main Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| PC Internet | Pages, information, websites | URL / webpage | Where is the information? |
| Mobile Internet | People, apps, location, services | App / account | What service can I use now? |
| AI Era | Humans, Agents, Skills, tools, data, tasks, reviews, reputation | Contribution event | Who contributed what, and what rights follow? |

The PC Internet made information accessible.

The Mobile Internet made services reachable.

The Contribution Internet makes contribution visible, verifiable, and allocatable.

## Why a New Internet-Like Layer Is Needed

AI changes the cost structure of creation.

Knowledge, code, documents, images, workflows, and decisions can be generated faster than before. But this creates a new problem: when output becomes abundant, trust becomes scarce.

The AI era needs a protocol layer that can answer:

- Who participated?
- What was contributed?
- Which AI, Agent, Skill, tool, or dataset helped?
- What evidence supports the claim?
- Who verified it?
- Who made the final human decision?
- What reputation, usage rights, or governance rights should follow?

PoCP exists to connect these relationships.

## What PoCP Connects

PoCP connects more than users and content.

It connects:

- humans;
- AI agents;
- skills;
- models;
- tools;
- datasets;
- workflows;
- tasks;
- sponsors;
- reviewers;
- communities;
- contribution records;
- reputation;

These are not just database objects. They are the nodes and edges of a new collaboration network.

## The Core Loop

```text
Contribution
  → Verification
  → CP
  → AI Credits
  → AI Use
  → More Contribution
```

This loop is the first working engine of the Contribution Internet.

It turns AI from a paywalled product into a shared capability that can be earned, governed, and improved through verified contribution.

## The Core Object: Contribution Event

The Contribution Internet needs a native object.

For the PC Internet, the native object was the webpage.

For the Mobile Internet, the native object was the app/service interaction.

For PoCP, the native object is the **Contribution Event**.

A Contribution Event records:

- the task;
- the primary contributor;
- all participating entities;
- their roles and weights;
- evidence;
- AI advisory verification;
- human review;
- reward issuance;
- ledger record.

This is how contribution becomes visible enough to support reputation, access rights, and eventually governance.

## The Contribution Graph

PoCP's long-term asset is the Contribution Graph:

```text
Human → uses → Agent
Agent → calls → Skill
Skill → invokes → Model
Tool → supports → Task
Dataset → informs → Contribution
AI Verifier → reviews → Contribution
Human Reviewer → approves → Contribution
Contribution → issues → AI Credits
Contribution → increases → Reputation
```

This graph can power:

- trust and discovery;
- fairer AI access;
- reviewer routing;
- skill and agent reputation;
- public-good incentives;
- sponsor impact reports;
- governance by contribution.

## What This Is Not

PoCP should not be described as only:

- an AI chatbot;
- a task board;
- a points system;
- a token project;
- a social credit system;
- a generic reputation app.

Those descriptions are too small.

PoCP is best understood as a protocol and application layer for verified contribution relationships in the AI era.

## Product Implication

If PoCP is the Contribution Internet, the MVP must prove one thing before everything else:

> A real contribution can move through the network and become verified access, reputation, and ledger memory.

That means the first product surface should prioritize:

1. Contribution submission.
2. Evidence structuring.
3. AI advisory verification.
4. Human review.
5. CP and AI Credits issuance.
6. Ledger and graph visibility.
7. Entity profiles for humans, Agents, and Skills.

Everything else is secondary until this loop feels alive.

## Final Line

The next internet may not be defined only by who publishes information or who consumes services.

It may be defined by who contributes value, how that contribution is verified, and how shared capability flows back to the contributors.

That is the network PoCP is trying to build.
