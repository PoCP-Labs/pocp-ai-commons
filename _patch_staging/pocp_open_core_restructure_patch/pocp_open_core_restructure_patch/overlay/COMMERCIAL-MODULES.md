# Commercial Modules

This document lists candidate commercial or restricted modules for PoCP.

## 1. Advanced Anti-Abuse Intelligence

Protects against:

- CP farming;
- AI Credits farming;
- Compute Credits farming;
- fake compute usage;
- collusive reviews;
- Sybil attacks;
- replay attacks;
- reputation manipulation;
- sponsor pool abuse.

May remain commercial / restricted.

Provides:

- AI-based routing optimization;
- enterprise SLA routing;
- cost optimization;
- capability ranking;
- compute provider selection;
- premium skill matching;
- risk-aware routing.

May remain commercial.

## 3. Managed Compute Scheduler

Provides:

- GPU provider matching;

- SLA enforcement;

May remain commercial.

May remain commercial.

## 5. Commercial API Gateway

- quotas;

- support tools.

May remain commercial.

## 6. Advanced Reputation and Risk Models

Provides:

- reviewer accuracy scoring;

May remain commercial / restricted.

## Principle

Commercial modules should not hide the protocol.

They should provide advanced intelligence, security, operations, and enterprise readiness.

PoCP begins with contribution.
