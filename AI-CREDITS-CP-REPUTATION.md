# AI Credits, CP, and Reputation

PoCP uses three early-stage rights and records:

```text
CP
AI Credits
Reputation
```

These are not the same.

## CP — Contribution Points

CP represents verified contribution value.

CP answers:

> How much contribution did this Entity create?

CP may be influenced by task difficulty, contribution quality, evidence strength, originality, impact, human review, risk, and participant role.

## AI Credits

AI Credits represent AI usage rights.

AI Credits answer:

> What AI capability can this contributor access?

A simple MVP formula may be:

```text
AI Credits = CP × 2
```

AI Credits can be used for AI Chat, AI assistance, AI verifier usage, Skill invocation, Agent invocation, and future PoCP capabilities.

AI Credits are not a token, not money, and not an investment product.

## Reputation

Reputation represents long-term contribution reliability.

Reputation answers:

> Should this Entity be trusted for future collaboration?

Reputation should be contextual.

Examples:

- Human: documentation reputation
- Human: code review reputation
- Agent: task execution reliability
- Skill: invocation success
- Reviewer: review consistency
- Organization: sponsor trust

## Relationship

```text
Contribution
→ Verification
→ CP
→ AI Credits
→ AI Use
→ More Contribution
→ Reputation
```

PoCP begins with contribution.
