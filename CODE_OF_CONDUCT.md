# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in PoCP AI Commons a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity, socioeconomic status, or background.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but be accountable for impact.
- Welcome newcomers.
- Give clear feedback.
- Credit others for their contributions.
- Disagree with ideas without attacking people.
- Be transparent when using AI assistance.
- Remember that AI is advisory, and humans remain responsible.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation, or abuse.
- Personal attacks.
- Doxxing or sharing private information.
- Discriminatory language.
- Spam, scams, or financial promotion.
- Token speculation or misleading financial claims.
- Attempts to manipulate contribution scores.
- Submitting work generated by AI without review or disclosure when disclosure is relevant.

## AI Participation

PoCP AI Commons allows humans, agents, skills, tools, and models to participate in contribution workflows.

However:

- AI cannot be used to harass others.
- AI-generated content must be reviewed by accountable human contributors.
- AI systems are not final authorities.
- Human reviewers remain responsible for final decisions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, PRs, or contributors that violate this Code of Conduct.

Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.

## Reporting

If you need to report behavior, contact the maintainers through GitHub issues or a private maintainer channel once available.

PoCP begins with contribution, and contribution requires trust.
