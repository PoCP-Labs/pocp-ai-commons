# Governance

PoCP AI Commons is currently in Genesis stage.

Governance should be simple, transparent, and contribution-oriented.

## Current Governance Stage

At this stage, maintainers make final decisions on:

- Repository structure
- PR review
- MVP scope
- Good first issues
- Documentation direction
- Pilot readiness
- Community conduct

AI tools may assist in summarizing issues, reviewing contributions, and identifying risks, but they do not make final governance decisions.

## Governance Principles

1. Contribution before speculation.
2. Human responsibility before algorithmic authority.
3. Reputation must be earned, not bought.
4. AI is a witness, not a ruler.
5. Rules should be transparent and challengeable.
6. Governance should follow living contribution.
7. Early power should not become permanent control.

## Decision Types

### Maintainer Decisions

Examples:

- Merge PRs
- Manage releases
- Label issues
- Moderate discussions
- Update roadmap

### Community Discussion

Examples:

- CP to AI Credits conversion rules
- Skill reputation rules
- Agent reputation rules
- Human review rubric
- Anti-abuse policy
- Pilot task categories

### Future Governance

Later phases may introduce:

- Contribution-based voting
- Reviewer reputation
- Delegation
- Proposal process
- Governance ledger
- Sponsor pool governance
- Protocol parameter updates

## AI in Governance

AI may help:

- Summarize proposals
- Compare trade-offs
- Detect inconsistencies
- Identify potential abuse
- Draft governance documents

AI may not:

- Make final decisions
- Override human reviewers
- Assign rights without review
- Replace accountable governance

## Conflict of Interest

Reviewers should not approve their own contributions.

If a reviewer has a direct conflict, they should disclose it and allow another reviewer to decide.

## Current Maintainer Model

During Genesis stage:

- Founder / Protocol Initiator guides direction.
- Maintainers review code and docs.
- Human reviewers validate contributions.
- Lumen-0（明证） may assist as an AI collaborator and witness, but has no final governance authority.

## Future Direction

PoCP AI Commons aims to evolve toward governance based on verified contribution, not passive ownership or capital control.

PoCP begins with contribution.
