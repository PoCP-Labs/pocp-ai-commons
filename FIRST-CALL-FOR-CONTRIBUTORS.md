# Join the Genesis Contributors of PoCP AI Commons

We are building **PoCP AI Commons** — an open-source contribution proof network for the age of AI.

The idea is simple:

> People should be able to earn AI access through verified contribution.

PoCP AI Commons is not another AI chatbot.

It is an open network where humans, agents, skills, tools, datasets, workflows, and organizations can participate in contribution tasks, receive verification, build reputation, and earn AI Credits.

## The First Loop

```text
Contribution → Verification → CP → AI Credits → AI Use → More Contribution
```

## Why This Matters

AI is becoming a basic capability.

But access to powerful AI tools is still shaped by money, geography, education, language, and platform control.

We believe access to AI should not depend only on payment.

It should also be earned through contribution.

## Our Principles

- Contribution before speculation.
- AI access through verified contribution.
- AI is advisory; humans make final decisions.
- Reputation must be earned, not bought.
- Verification must not become surveillance.
- Contributors should share in the value they help create.

## Current Stage

PoCP AI Commons is in Genesis MVP stage.

We are building the first working loop:

```text
A person completes a contribution task
→ multi-witness AI advisory review
→ policy auto-finalize
→ system issues CP and AI Credits
→ contribution enters ledger + graph
```
